The United States Remains in a League of Its Own

The US accounts for roughly 77.6% of all downloads across the top 20 countries — a figure largely reflecting PyPI traffic routed through US-based cloud infrastructure (AWS us-east-1, GCP us-central1, etc.) rather than pure end-user geography. Month-over-month, the US added over 11 billion downloads (+9.3%), consistent with sustained CI/CD pipeline and cloud workload expansion.

Notable Shifts vs. May 2026

🇸🇬 Singapore surges to #3 (+44.6%). The most dramatic move of the month. Singapore jumped from position #5 in May (3.73B) to #3 in June (5.39B), nearly matching Germany. This likely reflects expanded cloud footprints from hyperscalers in the Southeast Asian hub (AWS ap-southeast-1, GCP asia-southeast1), combined with the region's growing role as an AI inference hub.
🇬🇧 United Kingdom drops from #3 to #6 (−30%). The UK fell sharply from 5.17B to 3.63B downloads — a 1.54B reduction. This is an unusually large single-month contraction, likely reflecting a consolidation or migration of cloud workloads away from UK-region endpoints. Worth monitoring in July.
🇰🇷 South Korea climbs to #13 (+27.6%). Korea crossed the 1B monthly download threshold for the first time in the trailing months, growing from 835M to 1.07B. The growth aligns with Korea's expanding AI and semiconductor-adjacent development ecosystem.
🇨🇳 China contracts (−22.3%). China dropped from 1.11B to 865M, reversing the growth trend seen through Q1 2026. This may indicate increased use of domestic mirror networks rather than direct PyPI traffic.

3. Emerging Packages — Gaining Significant Traction

The following packages showed meaningful absolute download growth (May → June 2026) from an already-established base, or exhibited extraordinary proportional gains signalling rapid community adoption. Packages starting from negligible volumes (<1M/month) are excluded.

🚀 browser-use +743%

May: 23.6M June: 199.1M Absolute gain: +175M Growth ratio: 8.4×

The single most explosive move in June. browser-use is an AI agent framework for browser automation, allowing LLMs to control web browsers directly for agent workflows without APIs. Its 8.4× growth in a single month is remarkable — the absolute gain of 175M puts it on par with well-established mid-tier packages. The timing aligns with broader momentum around autonomous AI agents in Q2 2026. If adoption continues, it could crack the top-50 overall by Q3 2026.

🔬 sglang +67%

May: 303.9M June: 507.4M Absolute gain: +203M Growth ratio: 1.67×

SGLang (Structured Generation Language) is an LLM serving and inference engine from the LMSYS team. It recorded the largest absolute gain among emerging packages: +203M downloads. Moving from a research-adjacent tool to a production-grade inference runtime, SGLang is increasingly used alongside vLLM for high-throughput, structured-output LLM serving. Its growth is likely correlated with the Singapore and Korea download spikes — Southeast Asia is rapidly becoming a key region for LLM inference infrastructure.

📡 lmnr +522%

May: 15.1M June: 94.0M Absolute gain: +79M Growth ratio: 6.2×

Laminar is an open-source LLM observability and tracing platform. A 6.2× growth rate from a base of 15M downloads signals real adoption rather than a statistical anomaly. LLM observability is a crowded space, but Laminar's open-source positioning and SDK-first approach appear to be resonating. Its trajectory mirrors what was seen with the OpenTelemetry packages 18 months ago.

🤗 huggingface-hub +54%

May: 264.4M June: 407.6M Absolute gain: +143M Growth ratio: 1.54×

The HuggingFace Hub client continues its steady climb, adding 143M downloads in June alone and now firmly in the 400M+ monthly tier. The growth reflects expanding use of HuggingFace models in production pipelines — model fetching, dataset access, and space deployments are all funnelled through this client library.

🧪 pytest-json-ctrf +50%

May: 187.1M June: 280.9M Absolute gain: +94M Growth ratio: 1.50×

CTRF (Common Test Results Format) standardises test output across frameworks and languages. This plugin's 50% growth suggests rapid uptake in CI/CD pipelines needing unified test reporting — particularly in multi-language or AI-assisted development environments where tooling standardisation is a priority.

📦 hatchling +30%

May: 320.6M June: 417.2M Absolute gain: +97M Growth ratio: 1.30×

The build backend for the Hatch project management tool grows steadily at +30% MoM, driven by increasing migration from legacy setuptools-based workflows. Projects adopting pyproject.toml-only configurations are the key adoption vector.

Summary

June 2026 tells a clear story: AI-native tooling is becoming PyPI's fastest-growing category, while the foundational infrastructure layer (HTTP, serialisation, AWS SDKs) remains deeply entrenched. Geographically, Singapore's sharp rise and the UK's steep contraction are the most significant signals to track in July — both are likely cloud-infrastructure artefacts rather than shifts in developer population, but the direction and magnitude are unusual.

The convergence of browser-use, sglang, lmnr, huggingface-hub, and the OpenTelemetry ecosystem all growing simultaneously in the same month suggests that agentic AI application development is entering a new phase of production deployment — not just prototyping.
